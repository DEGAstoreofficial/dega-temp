Free Your Games

DEGA is a censorship-resistant, permissionless game store. Sellers keep 100% of every sale and can't be cut off. Buyers keep a receipt that proves what they bought, forever. We are not the middleman, just the place both sides meet.

Sellers run their own store

Your games will appear on DEGA, as well as your own site if desired, removing DEGA from being a single point of failure.

02

Buyers pay the seller directly

No cut, no intermediary account. New or traditional payment rails, whichever the seller sets up.

03

The receipt is the proof

An encrypted receipt is attached to your account and can't be removed. Never lose it, never lose your right to a copy of a game.
